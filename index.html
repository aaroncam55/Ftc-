      How values work
      1. Pulls Sleeper's projections for every remaining week and scores them with your league's scoring settings.
      2. Recent form compares each player's last 4 weeks of real points with his projection and nudges his value up or down, by at most 25%.
      3. Replacement level at each position is the 2nd best player on your league's waiver wire. Season value is rest-of-season points above that.
      4. Values run 0 to 1,000, where 1,000 is the most valuable player in your league.
      5. Keeper value is next year's value (DynastyProcess open data) minus the value of the round he'd cost: 1 round earlier than this season, or the last round if undrafted.
      6. Draft picks: next year's order is random, so each pick is worth the average player in its round. Only clear-cut keepers (Strong or Elite value) are assumed off the board, and that is counted at half strength, since teams don't always keep their best players. Picks 2 or more seasons out count at 75%. Picks only count in the Season + keepers view.
      7. A keeper can only be kept with the team's own original pick in his cost round, and only one keeper per round. Picks acquired in trades can't be used. If a team trades away its own pick in that round, the keeper value is lost, and the trade counts that loss.
      8. The star premium makes one great player worth more than two good ones. Auto sets it from your league's size.
      9. Your ranking adjustments scale a player's value by a percentage. When the community database is connected, everyone's adjustments are shared anonymously and the Community view uses the median adjustment for each player.
      10. Lineup impact rebuilds each team's best starting lineup after the trade, including drops forced by roster limits.
      Data from the public Sleeper API and DynastyProcess open data. Reload to refresh projections and stats.
